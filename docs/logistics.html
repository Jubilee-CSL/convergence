Travel

By Air

Paris is served by two main international airports:

Paris-Charles de Gaulle (CDG) — ~35 km north-east. Take the RER B direct (approx. 40 min, €14). Exit at Denfert-Rochereau, then Metro 4 south to Mairie de Montrouge.

Paris-Orly (ORY) — ~18 km south. Take the Orlyval shuttle to Antony, then RER B north to Denfert Rochereau (approx. 35 min total, €14).

By Rail

Paris has six mainline terminals. Travellers from London (Eurostar), Brussels or Amsterdam arrive at Gare du Nord. Take Metro 4 southbound to Mairie de Montrouge (approx. 30 min).

From Gare de Lyon (TGV from southern France, Italy, Switzerland): take Metro 14 to Châtelet–Les Halles, then Metro 4 southbound to Mairie de Montrouge.

Local Transport

A Navigo Easy card (available at any metro station, €2) or single t+ tickets cover all metro, RER (within Paris), and bus travel. A single journey costs €2.15.

Cycling via Vélib' (public bike-share) is a convenient option — stations near the venue.
